OmniPOCUS AI — Git Repository & System Architecture Blueprint

Open-Source Vendor-Neutral AI-POCUS Integration Specification (ITKPOCUS + MONAI + ONNX WebGPU)

The Silo Problem

Cardiac, Lung, and OB AI models are currently locked inside proprietary hardware tablets, forcing clinicians to juggle separate probes, apps, and unlinked patient records.

Unified Ingestion Layer

Ingests raw frames via OpenIGTLink, RTSP, UVC, or DICOM from Butterfly, Clarius, GE, Philips, and Sonosite into a normalized 512×512 tensor pipeline.

Cross-Organ Synthesis

Combines outputs across cardiac, thoracic, abdominal, and fetal AI modules into a single HL7 FHIR R4 Observation bundle & DICOM-SR report.
